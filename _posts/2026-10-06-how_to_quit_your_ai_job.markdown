*Note: What follows is satire that was produced completely with my brain and my brain only… for better or for worse.* 

&nbsp;

Hello there! Are you an AI leader that is paid a disgusting amount of money to the point you have no idea what life is like for the average person all so that you can help build a technology that struggles to identify bananas but will supposedly kill us all? If yes, perfect this article is for you. If no, this article can still serve as good to know knowledge in case you by chance become an AI leader that is paid a disgusting amount of money to the point that you no longer have any idea what life is like for the average person all so that you can help build a technology that struggles to identify bananas but will supposedly kill us all! So either way, you should read this!

&nbsp;

Okay, so if you read that, either you’re 1) an AI researcher who is paid a disgusting amount of money to build a killing machine or 2) you’re someone who might one day be paid a disgusting amount of money to - build a killing machine. That’s awesome for you. The thing is, if you’re in the former category, this job is probably weighing on your conscience heavily. “Am I really helping psychopaths like Sam Allbad and Dario Adorko build something that might kill all humans just for a nice paycheck? How can I go to sleep at night?” Well to answer that question, I’d recommend a nice mattress. Anyway, if you’re in the latter category, although you might think “man I’d be willing to destroy all of humanity if it meant making a disgusting amount of money,” don’t be fooled - you too will eventually find yourself compelled to agonize about whether it is a good thing to be helping out psychopaths like Sam Allbad and Dario Adorko. It’s pretty much guaranteed you’ll wake up one day and realize that your grandiose sense of self importance necessitates that you obnoxiously inform the world of your morals and how they require you to resign from your position of making a disgusting amount of money.  Sending out your treatise decrying the psychopaths at the top is a delicate task though that should not be done in haste. Whether you send out your resignation letter to the world that no asked for via Elon Musk’s sad version of Twitter or you have your friends in the media publish your slop, you wanna get this right. What follows are some pointers to help assure you make the most of your declaration to the world and come off as the most virtuous of humans.

&nbsp;

First things first. At this point, as important as you think you are, you are not the first to do this. Sorry, I know - your mom told you that you are special and now you have to accept you are not. The fact is, this stunt has been pulled a non trivial number of times. So sadly you’re gonna have to acknowledge that. Unlike AI, it will help you to show the goodwill to acknowledge the shoulders you stand on. A good trope I personally recommend is saying something like “at this point it’s a cliche to write this, but [insert your oversized sense of self importance and morality here].”

&nbsp;

Second, immediately after admitting you are not the first idiot to embarrass yourself like this, it is absolutely essential you add the following somewhere in your intro: “I did not make this decision lightly.” You can feel free to adapt this a little to your needs, but don’t screw with it too much. It’s absolutely critical that people know you are really torn between continuing to earn a disgusting amount of money versus not continuing to earn a disgusting amount of money. Making it clear to your reader up front that you struggle to know whether it is a good thing to get paid a lot money to build something that could kill everyone will really help them emphasize with you. And God knows you are impoverished in terms of empathy people have for you.

&nbsp;

Third, you really ought to let your readers know pretty quickly that this isn’t just a marketing stunt for AI. Sure, your “I regret taking in large sums of money to build something that maybe idk possibly could kill us…*cough* because it’s so cool and super powerful and super useful *cough*” resignation letter might draw more attention to AI, but obviously that is not the point. You are putting yourself out there despite that risk! You’re really a hero when you think about it. The problem is, we live in a post literate world - people these days aren’t good at reading between the lines. So it’s really important you make it explicit to the reader that this isn’t meant to hype AI more - but rather, it’s purely for you to stroke your own ego and virtue signal that you are a disgustingly rich individual who really cares about humanity! So disgustingly rich that you have the luxury to just quit your job on a random day of the week and tell the entire world about it while you call your former employer evil!

&nbsp;

Once you have made sure to touch on the first three points early on, a large portion of the middle is mostly up to whatever specific morals led you to this hard decision that you personally want to have your reader suffer through hearing you wax on about. Even so, there are a plethora of themes and points that will behoove you to drop in along the way. For instance, definitely work in a mention about your colleagues who earned disgusting amounts of money alongside of you being good and smart people. Bonus points if you add that they really do have humanity’s best interests in mind (it’s just the promise of more disgusting amounts of money, you know, it makes things hard for them, okay?! But like, if not for all the money they’d be really good people!) In addition, you should strive to note that you really- no like *really* - believe that if only the people earning disgusting amounts of money would build AI with safety in mind, that AI would be an amazing technology that makes the world better off. And if you are feeling particularly audacious, do consider tossing in something about how you would give up all your money if it meant saving the world. People love such hypotheticals that would never actually play out in the real world! Speaking of things people love, they love a good story. So if you have some kind of example where you saw your company AI do a very evil and screwed up thing, throw that in too. Even better if you can serve it up as the inspiration for your conscience to start questioning if all the money is worth it!

&nbsp;

Let’s pause for a moment - I know this is a lot to remember. So much so you’re probably tempted to toss this over to your company AI to have it summarize this for you. While tempting, stay with me here - it’s not every day you’re gonna quit your job and force the world to read about all your grave concerns! You owe it to yourself to inject what little humanity you have into this! In fairness though I know it’s a lot of to dos. So to spare you another, let me give you a do not: Although you should feel free to express all the regrets you want, what you definitely should not do is apologize. Apologies are for forgetting your SO’s birthday because you were too busy flirting with a chatbot - they aren’t for building something that can kill the entire species. The former is personal whereas the latter is just the cost of a good paycheck - no need to apologize for that!

&nbsp;

Now, at some point you will have droned on long enough about your grave concerns and cognitive dissonance that you’ll realize it’s time to wrap things up. Like with anything you’re really gonna wanna stick this landing. To do so, first, it’s important you include a call to action. All your other colleagues who have done this song and dance have done the same. And given how successful their call to actions have been, obviously you want to follow in their brave footsteps! Be sure your call to action, though, is vague and pointless - for example, saying something general like “We should strive to get AI companies to commit to safety” - that’s perfect! What you don’t want to do is provide any real actionable solutions. To do so would be to risk actually maybe making things a teensy bit better - and that’s not your goal here. The goal is all about making yourself feel like a good little egg.

&nbsp;

After you’ve made your call to action, the final step is to end with a profound sounding embrace of “humanity.” This is imperative - don’t skimp on this or treat it as optional. Look I get it, you’re probably a lizard person who deep down has no  capacity to understand, let alone care for, humanity in the slightest. But you’re gonna have to put on your best acting face here and go all out. The fluffier and more profoundy you make this embrace sound, the better. If you have trouble expressing emotion through your written word, this may be the one place to get consider some help from your favorite chatbot - as it’s probably guaranteed it can feign emotion far better than you ever could. Regardless, whether it comes from your dark void of a soul or a robot, you don’t even need to worry about it actually being meaningful. As long as it sounds like you go to sleep every night wiping tears from your face with the money you earned building a technology that struggles to identify bananas but may kill us all because your love for humanity causes your cognitive dissonance to force an emotion out of you, you’re golden!
